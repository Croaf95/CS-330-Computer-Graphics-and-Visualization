## CS 330 Portfolio Reflection

# How do I approach designing software?

I approach software design by first identifying the project requirements and determining how the individual components will work together to achieve the desired outcome. Throughout this course, I learned the importance of planning before coding and breaking larger problems into smaller, manageable tasks. By creating a design plan before implementation, I was able to stay organized and make more effective decisions during development.

# What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?

This project helped me develop skills in 3D scene design, spatial organization, object placement, and visual composition. I gained experience balancing aesthetics and functionality while creating a scene that met project requirements and remained visually engaging.

# What design process did you follow for your project work?

My design process began with reviewing the project requirements and creating a plan for the scene layout. I identified the objects I wanted to include, determined their placement, and then progressively added textures, lighting, and camera controls. After each milestone, I reviewed the results and made adjustments to improve the overall design.

# How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?

The planning and iterative design approach I used in this project can be applied to future software projects by helping me organize complex requirements into smaller tasks. This process improves efficiency, reduces errors, and makes projects easier to maintain as they grow in complexity.

# How do I approach developing programs?

I approach program development by building functionality incrementally and testing frequently. Rather than attempting to implement everything at once, I focus on creating a working foundation and then adding new features in stages. This allows me to identify and resolve issues early in the development process.

# What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?

One strategy I used was implementing and testing features one at a time. I developed individual scene components, textures, lighting effects, and camera controls separately before integrating them into the final project. This made debugging easier and ensured each feature worked correctly before moving on.

# How did iteration factor into your development?

Iteration played a significant role throughout the project. I continuously modified object placement, lighting, textures, and scene details based on testing and visual evaluation. Each milestone built on the previous work, allowing me to refine both the functionality and appearance of the scene.

# How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones, which led you to the project’s completion?

As I progressed through the milestones, I became more comfortable with OpenGL concepts and graphical programming techniques. My development approach evolved from focusing solely on functionality to considering code organization, readability, and maintainability. I gained confidence in making modifications and troubleshooting issues throughout the project.

# How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

Computer science provides me with problem-solving, analytical, and technical skills that support my long-term career goals in software development and technology. The knowledge gained through my coursework helps me build practical solutions, adapt to new technologies, and continue growing as a professional in the field.

# How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future educational pathway?

Computational graphics and visualization have expanded my understanding of mathematical concepts, rendering techniques, object transformations, lighting, and visual communication. These skills will support future coursework involving software development, game development, simulation, and user interface design.

# How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future professional pathway?

Professionally, computational graphics and visualization have provided me with experience creating interactive visual applications and understanding how graphical systems are developed. These skills can be applied in software engineering, game development, simulation systems, data visualization, and other technical fields that rely on presenting information through visual interfaces. This project also strengthened my ability to design, develop, test, and refine complex software solutions, which are valuable skills in any technology-related career.
